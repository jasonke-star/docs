## Quick Start

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/auto/homepage.png" alt="PartGenie homepage with Search, Work, Optimize BOM, Design, and Tariff lookup" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Open [app.partgenie.ai](https://app.partgenie.ai) and sign in with email, Google, or LinkedIn.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/en-US/login.png" alt="PartGenie Login Interface" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Set role and industry so recommendations fit your work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First search">
    Stay on **Search** and try an MPN or natural-language ask:

    ```
    STM32F103C8T6 operating voltage
    alternative to BQ24195
    100uF 16V electrolytic capacitor
    ```

    Use **voice**, file/image attach, or suggested prompts under the box.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Homepage modes

| Control | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Component Q&A, alternatives, citations |
| **Work** | Analyze/act on an attached BOM or Design project |
| **Optimize BOM** | Jump into BOM health / risk flows |
| **Design Hardware** | Start Design (Beta) |
| **Tariff lookup** | US import tariff estimate by origin |

Details: [AI Search](/en-US/ai-search) · [PartGenie Work](/en-US/partgenie-work)

## After your first answer

1. Click **MPN / page citation badges** to verify sources — [Datasheet citations](/en-US/datasheet-citations)
2. Open the component drawer → **Procurement** for live stock/price — [Live sourcing](/en-US/live-sourcing)
3. Try a follow-up, or **Design** from chat — [Design mode](/en-US/design-mode)

## First BOM in minutes

1. Switch to **Work** (or use Optimize BOM) and upload CSV/Excel / paste a part list
2. Ask: `Analyze top risks from this BOM`
3. Review health, Adopt alternatives, check **Changes** and **Est. price**

Guides: [BOM management](/en-US/bom-management) · [Feature examples](/en-US/feature-examples)

## Sidebar essentials

- **Projects** — BOM & Design workspaces
- **Knowledge** — your files + AI artifacts
- **Collection** — saved parts
- **Credits** (header) — balance, Top up, plan — [Membership](/en-US/membership-pricing)

## Settings worth opening early

- **Language** — Auto or a specific locale
- **Distributors** — which live sources appear
- Notifications — email / in-app preferences
