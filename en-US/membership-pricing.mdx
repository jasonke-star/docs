## How usage works: Credits

Usage is measured in **credits**, not a single search counter. Different workflows—component search, alternatives, BOM analysis, Design—consume credits at different rates.

- Credits **reset monthly**
- Free includes a monthly allotment (shown in the top-bar credits panel)
- **Top up** anytime without waiting for the monthly reset
- Check balance from the credits indicator in the header

## Pricing snapshot

Exact quotas and list prices can change—always confirm in-app billing. Typical public reference:

- **Free** — $0 / month, limited monthly credits
- **Pro** — monthly or annual (annual often discounted); unlocks Pro-only capabilities such as advanced export
- **Team / Enterprise** — custom
