PartGenie search is built for hardware engineers—not generic chat. You get **verifiable answers** grounded in component data, datasheets, and live sources.

## What you can ask

- **MPN lookup** — exact part numbers, near matches, manufacturer suggestions
- **Specs & applications** — voltage, package, use-case driven selection
- **Alternatives** — replacements with project context (see [Alternatives](/en-US/alternatives))
- **Modules & kits** — development boards and robotics parts via [OpenELAB](/en-US/openelab-modules)

```
Examples:
- "STM32F103C8T6 operating voltage"
- "alternative to BQ24195 for 4.5A USB charging"
- "100uF 16V electrolytic, automotive grade"
```

## How to search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the homepage">
    Go to [app.partgenie.ai](https://app.partgenie.ai). Use **Search** mode for component Q&A, or **Work** when you're acting on a BOM/Design project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a query">
    Type naturally, paste an MPN, upload a datasheet/image, or use **voice input** (microphone in the search bar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the answer">
    Answers include structured part cards, tables, and **citation badges**. Gray MPN badges open the component drawer; green = OEM, orange = distributor, purple = community.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    Use suggested follow-ups—including one-click **Design** when you want to start hardware design from a part.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verifiable answers

Most AI tools give you text. PartGenie cites **exact MPNs** and **web sources** so you can check before you design-in:

- Click an MPN badge → component drawer (specs, stock, pricing)
- Click distributor badges → jump to source listings
- Datasheet claims can include **page-level citations** (see [Datasheet citations](/en-US/datasheet-citations))
