## Product

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is PartGenie?">
    An AI electronics copilot for selection, BOM/Design projects, live sourcing, and verifiable answers (MPN citations, datasheet page evidence, web sources).

    Start here: [Introduction](/en-US/index) · [Getting started](/en-US/getting-started)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who is it for?">
    Hardware/design engineers, procurement & supply chain, FAE/applications, makers and students.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is it different from a generic search engine or LLM?">
    PartGenie is grounded in component databases, datasheets, and distributor feeds. Answers cite MPNs and sources you can open and verify—not free-form text alone. See [AI Search](/en-US/ai-search).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Search vs Work?">
    **Search** — open-ended component Q&A, alternatives, tariff. **Work** — analyze and change an attached BOM or Design project. See [PartGenie Work](/en-US/partgenie-work).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can BOM do today?">
    Upload CSV/Excel, health report, risk flags, bulk alternatives, Adopt, engineering Changes, estimated price, compare, share. See [BOM management](/en-US/bom-management).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Alternatives & compare?">
    Context-aware substitute search, MPN compare with live sourcing, and exportable Alternative reports (PDF/Word). See [Alternatives](/en-US/alternatives) and [Product comparison](/en-US/product-comparison).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Live pricing / which distributors?">
    DigiKey, LCSC, TME, Unikeyic, OnlineComponents, WinSource, Arrow, and more—toggle under Settings → Distributors. One-click **Purchase** jumps to the distributor page. See [Live sourcing](/en-US/live-sourcing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mobile apps?">
    **Android is live** (snap parts, scan PCBs, SMD codes). **iOS is coming soon**. Web remains the fullest experience. See [Mobile app](/en-US/mobile-app).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I share chats or BOMs?">
    Yes—invite by email (invitees get mail) or copy a link; private or public access. See [Share](/en-US/share).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data & trust

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where does data come from?">
    Large component catalog, manufacturer materials, and live/catalog distributor feeds. Modules/kits also via [OpenELAB](/en-US/openelab-modules).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I verify an answer?">
    Click MPN badges, distributor badges, or datasheet **page** citations; open Procurement for live stock/price. See [Datasheet citations](/en-US/datasheet-citations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my project data private?">
    Your projects, BOMs, and Knowledge files are private to your account unless you explicitly share them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
