title: "Effective BOM Management Best Practices"
description: "Keep BOMs healthy—match, risk, adopt, diff, cost, and share"
---

## Ingest cleanly

- Prefer CSV/Excel with a clear MPN column; paste lists when files aren’t ready
- Resolve unmatched lines early—everything downstream depends on Full Match quality

## Review health before buying

- Check distribution + risk badges (NRND/EOL, zero stock, missing manufacturer)
- Use bulk alternatives, then **Adopt** deliberately—not every suggestion is drop-in

## Track engineering change

- Use **Changes** for Adopt history and version diffs before you freeze a revision
- Watch **Est. price** as you swap lines so cost impact stays visible

## Work with the agent

- In **Work**, reference specific BOM lines when asking sourcing questions
- Multi-select → **Compare** before locking a dual-source strategy

## Collaborate

- Share the BOM (email invite) instead of exporting screenshots alone
- Keep supporting PDFs in Knowledge; keep the line list in Projects
