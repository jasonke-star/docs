When PartGenie quotes electrical characteristics, ratings, or operating conditions, it can attach **page-level evidence** from the datasheet.

## How it works

1. Ask a component question (e.g. *STM32F103C8T6 operating voltage*)
2. Spec values appear with a **page number badge**
3. Click the badge to open the datasheet PDF at that page
4. Verify the table or paragraph, then continue the conversation
