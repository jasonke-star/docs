Open **Settings** from your avatar (sidebar / header).

## Profile

Update nickname, avatar, role, and industry so recommendations stay relevant.

## Security & login

- **Email** — bind / change with verification code
- **Password** — set or change under account & password
- **Google / LinkedIn** (and region-specific methods such as phone/WeChat where available) — bind for faster login
- Forgot password — use in-app reset, or email [support@partgenie.ai](mailto:support@partgenie.ai)

More failure cases: [Account troubleshooting](/en-US/account-troubleshooting)

## Language

**Settings → Language**: pick a locale or **Auto** (follow browser). Supported locales include English, Deutsch, 日本語, 한국어, 繁體中文, Français, Español (México).

## Distributors (live sourcing)

**Settings → Distributors**: toggle DigiKey, LCSC, TME, Unikeyic, OnlineComponents, WinSource, Arrow, and other sources. Preferences apply across component detail, BOM, and compare. See [Live sourcing](/en-US/live-sourcing).

## Credits & billing

- Header **Credits** panel — monthly balance, Top up, upgrade
- Plans and Pro **7-day trial** — [Membership & pricing](/en-US/membership-pricing)

## Notifications

Configure email / in-app alerts and completion sounds where available (notifications center / settings).

## Sharing & privacy

Shared chats/BOMs are controlled from each item's **Share** dialog—not global account settings. See [Share](/en-US/share).
