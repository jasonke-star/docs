Comparison in PartGenie is built for sourcing and design decisions: pick candidates, then evaluate them together with live data when available.

## Where to compare

- **Search / alternative answers** — select products in tables or result cards
- **BOM** — multi-select lines and click **Compare**
- **Alternative report** — structured shortlist you can export (PDF/Word)

See [Alternatives](/en-US/alternatives) for clarification prompts, relation types (upgrade path / functional alternate), and report export.

## What the compare view shows

Typical columns include stock, price (with MOQ), lead time, confidence / relation notes, and distributor source. Use **Purchase** from procurement when you're ready to buy—see [Live sourcing](/en-US/live-sourcing).

## Tips

- Compare **2–5** candidates; more than that usually hurts clarity
- Confirm package, firmware, and electrical limits before design-in—catalog similarity ≠ drop-in
- Export tables from answers when on **Pro** (hover table → download)
