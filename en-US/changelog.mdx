  Alternative search no longer stops at parametric matching. When you ask for a replacement, PartGenie now **asks clarifying questions**—power limits, thermal constraints, package fit, application scenarios—to capture your project context before recommending parts.

<Update label="2026-08-11" tags={["Tariff", "Compliance", "Beta"]} description="Tariff lookup — MPN-level US import tariff (Beta)">
  <Frame>
    <img
      alt="Tariff lookup homepage input with country of origin selector showing China, Taiwan, Vietnam and other major origins"
      src="/images/tariff-country-select.png"
    />
  </Frame>

  <Frame>
    <img
      alt="Tariff result for 74HC595 made in China: HTS 8542, base rate 0%, Section 301 China semiconductor 50%, forced labor surcharge 12.5%, estimated total 62.5%"
      src="/images/tariff-result.png"
    />
  </Frame>

  **Tariff Lookup is now in Beta.**

  In 2026, supply chain decisions can't ignore trade policy. Tariffs are shifting fast—and for electronics, the complexity is real: thousands of part numbers, ambiguous HTS classifications, and layered surcharges that stack on top of each other. Getting it wrong affects sourcing decisions, cost models, and compliance.

  PartGenie now lets you check the **estimated US import tariff for a specific MPN by country of origin**. Enter a part number, select where it's made, and get a full tariff breakdown—base rate, applicable Section 301 surcharges, UFLPA rates, and an estimated total—all in one place.

  > Estimate only — not a binding customs determination. Verify with a licensed customs broker before filing.

  Currently supports **US import tariff** lookup. More destination countries are on the way—tell us which you need most.
</Update>
