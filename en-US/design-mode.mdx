**Design** turns a hardware idea into a reviewable pack: brief, plan (with schemes you pick), architecture, an initial BOM, and images. It is still Beta.

## Start a design

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Design">
    On the homepage, click **Design Hardware**, or choose **Work** and describe the product. You can also use a **Design** follow-up under a component answer to start from that part.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the product">
    Say what it is, the constraints that matter, and who it is for. Example: a robot vacuum with a height limit, LiDAR, and a mid-range cost target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the stages">
    The canvas lists the stages as they finish: **Brief → Plan → Architecture → BOM → Image**. A green check means that stage is complete. Click a stage to open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review the plan

**Plan** is where you choose a direction before the rest of the pack is treated as final.

The plan page shows:

- **Goals** — what the hardware must do
- **Metrics** — numbers such as height, suction, battery, charge current
- **Constraints** — certification, cost, environment, power source, size
- **Schemes** — more than one architecture. One is marked **Active**

The note on schemes: *Pick a scheme as the direction. Ask in chat to change the options.*

Use the stage tabs at the top (**Plan**, and the counts next to architecture / BOM / images). **Use** applies the selection. **Export** downloads the pack.

## Images

The **Image** stage can show more than one view. The large frame is the current view (often labeled **Hero**). Thumbnails underneath switch angle. Click a thumbnail to review that view.

## After the pack exists

- The initial BOM from Design can be opened and checked like any other BOM: health, live stock, alternatives. See [BOM management](/en-US/bom-management).
- Parts you will layout need ECAD separately: catalog files on **CAD Models**, or a generated library. See [ECAD & footprints](/en-US/ecad-models) and [KiCad library generation](/en-US/kicad-library).
- Design spends **credits**. See [Membership & pricing](/en-US/membership-pricing).
