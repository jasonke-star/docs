**Design mode (Beta)** helps you move from idea to structured hardware deliverables inside PartGenie.

## What you can do

- Start a **Design project** from the homepage chip or Projects
- Generate **concept design**, architecture diagrams, initial BOM, and technical proposals
- Trigger design from chat: after an AI answer on a part, use the **Design** follow-up to jump into hardware design with that part as context

## Quotas & libraries

Design uses your plan **credits**. Hardware packs, agent-assisted edits, and ECAD library support may vary by plan—check [Membership & pricing](/en-US/membership-pricing).
