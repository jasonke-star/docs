PartGenie treats a BOM as a living engineering document—match lines, flag risks, adopt alternatives, track changes, and estimate cost.

## Create or upload

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach the BOM">
    On the homepage, click **Optimize BOM** or switch the input to **Work**, then upload **CSV / Excel** or paste a part list. You can also open an existing BOM from **Projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the toolbar">
    Across the top of the BOM:

    - **Refreshing live stock** — how many lines have been priced
    - **Files & Rules** — project files and instructions (these override your account custom instructions)
    - **Changes** — Adopt history and version diff
    - **Health** — score for the current BOM
    - **Est.** — estimated cost range and how many lines are priced
    - **Export** / **Share**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work the table">
    Each row shows the original part number, **Match** (for example Full Match), **Stock / Source** distributor marks, and brand. Filter with **Level** and **Match**. **Add Data Row** inserts a line. Row actions copy, duplicate, or delete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Health report

After upload, open the health overview for:

- **Component distribution** by category (with risk counts)
- **Risk list** — NRND/EOL, zero stock, no sourcing data, missing manufacturer
- **Bulk alternatives** — scan matched lines and surface functional replacements

## Engineering diff & estimated price

In the BOM toolbar:

- **Changes** — Adopt history and version history (what replaced what, when lines changed)
- **Est. price** — BOM-level cost estimate from live sourcing (e.g. range + how many lines priced)
- **Health** — overall health score

## Compare & chat context

- Select rows → **Compare** for side-by-side sourcing
- Multi-select BOM lines and reference them in agent chat for sourcing decisions
