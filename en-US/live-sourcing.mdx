PartGenie pulls **live price and stock** from major distributors into component detail, BOM, compare views, and search/alternative results.

## Supported live sources

Current live pricing sources include (toggle any under **Settings → Distributors**):

- DigiKey, LCSC, TME, Unikeyic, OnlineComponents, WinSource, Arrow

Catalog snapshots may appear for sources without live feeds yet.

## Where you see it

- **Component Procurement tab** — price tiers, packaging, update time
- **BOM / Compare** — stock and price columns
- **Search & alternatives** — live sourcing rows with confidence and source

## One-click purchase

On the Procurement tab, click **Purchase** to jump to that distributor's product page and order.

## Preferences

Your distributor toggles apply across all views. Want another source? Tell us—we're continuously adding distributors. Manufacturer program partnerships are expanding as well.
