PartGenie pulls **live price and stock** from major distributors into component detail, BOM, compare views, and search/alternative results.

## Supported live sources

Current live pricing sources include (toggle any under **Settings → Distributors**):

- DigiKey, LCSC, TME, Unikeyic, OnlineComponents, WinSource, Arrow

Catalog snapshots may appear for sources without live feeds yet.

## Where you see it

- **Component Procurement tab** — price tiers, packaging, update time
- **BOM / Compare** — stock and price columns
- **Search & alternatives** — live sourcing rows with confidence and source

## One-click purchase

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the drawer">
    Click an MPN badge in an answer, or a part row in the BOM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Procurement">
    The drawer tabs are **Product**, **Procurement**, and **CAD Models**. Stay on **Procurement** for price and stock. **CAD Models** is the footprint library — see [ECAD & footprints](/en-US/ecad-models).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a package break">
    Under the distributor name (DigiKey, TME, LCSC, and so on), switch Tape & Reel, Cut Tape, or the equivalent the distributor offers. The price grid updates by quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase">
    Click **Purchase** to open that distributor's product page. The timestamp next to it is when PartGenie last refreshed the quote.
  </Step>
</Steps>
