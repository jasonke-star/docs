You can **share** chat answers and BOMs with teammates.

## How to share

1. Open a chat session or BOM and click **Share**
2. Enter emails (comma-separated) and choose permission (e.g. **Can view**)
3. Click **Invite** — invitees receive an **email notification**
4. Or **Copy link** to share directly

## Access levels

- **Only invited people** — private to you and invitees
- **Public access** — anyone with the link can view

Owner keeps **Full access**. Adjust invites anytime from the Share dialog.
